## Evaluation regime

- Scientific diagnostics first for claims with faithful intermediate tests:
  per-layer parameter changes, activation variance/effective rank, temporal
  slowness, layerwise probe accuracy, and train/probe loss.
- Blind validation-only comparison for optimization and architecture choices.
- Downstream metric: MNIST validation accuracy from a matched linear readout.
  The primary readout concatenates all hidden layers for TeReL, random, and
  supervised encoders; a last-layer readout is a secondary diagnostic.
- Direction: maximize validation accuracy while rejecting collapsed candidates.
- Parser: JSON field `metrics.accuracy`; diagnostic fields under
  `representation_diagnostics`, `class_structure_diagnostics`, and
  `encoder_training`.

## Commands

- Tests: `uv run pytest tests/test_resubmission_*.py -q`
- Experiment: `uv run python -m terel.resubmission.recovery --config configs/resubmission/recovery-v2.yaml --candidate <id> --seed <seed>`

## Common baseline

Corrected protocol-v1 TeReL: 512--256 LeakyReLU encoder, detached local
objective, jointly trained for 10 epochs, no augmentation or normalization,
and a last-layer linear probe. Re-run seed 11 under the recovery runner before
interventions. The earlier validation ledger reports 0.882 mean accuracy and
the held-out diagnostic reports 0.886 mean accuracy; neither is the recovery
target.

## Budget and stopping rule

- Up to 24 one-seed recovery candidates, followed by at most four finalists
  over three validation seeds and at most two final configurations over five
  frozen confirmatory seeds.
- Up to 12 GPU-hours for recovery selection before reassessing the search
  design. Stop sooner after corrected TeReL reaches at least 97% mean MNIST
  validation accuracy and lies within two percentage points of its matched
  backprop control, provided no collapse gate fails.
- Competitive performance is a hard revision target; exhausting this initial
  budget triggers a documented diagnosis and a newly bounded recovery stage,
  not automatic claim retirement.
